## Books, illustrations and catalogue

Source: Aozora Bunko (青空文庫), https://www.aozora.gr.jp/
Usage rules: https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html

Each immutable books/*.json descriptor retains its official work/card/source URLs, authors and contributors, source revision, catalogue identity, bibliography, input/proofreading credits, and conversion provenance. Follow the per-book source link for its original text and notices. Aozora catalogue metadata is attributed to 青空文庫 under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . This attribution does not place the original literary works or illustrations under a new licence.

The collection contains 17,344 successfully converted works (811,195 pages) from a 17,352-entry selection. Eight unavailable or incomplete sources were excluded rather than represented with missing material. Selection used the official work and contributor copyright-expired flags for Japan; this is not a claim of public-domain status in every jurisdiction. Book content and source contributors' rights are separate from PDFReader product code and its licence.

Conversions include vector typography, vertical layout, ruby, illustrations and supported source styling. Shared-font mapping, lossless PNG recompression and compact records change storage without removing body content. Known source markup corrections are recorded in the affected descriptors; work 2519 has its malformed H1/H2 outline boundary corrected while retaining the body. Report conversion problems with the work ID and page.
